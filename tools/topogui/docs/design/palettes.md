# TopoGUI palette candidates

Colour palettes collected by the owner for future trials of the visual
direction (development plan, "Visual direction"). The `BIAS` lines of
the source were deliberately ignored. Each palette lists its colours
as they may map onto the design tokens of `src/topogui-theme.ads`
(`Surface`, `Edge_Shade`, `Slate_Text`, ...); the mapping is to be
decided at trial time, not here.
